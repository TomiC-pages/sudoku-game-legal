Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Tom ("we", "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use Sudoku Game (the "App"), available on Android under the package identifier com.tomic.sudoku_game.

By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to operate the App.

Account & profile data

  • Firebase UID — a unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication. Anonymous users receive a randomly assigned UID; users who sign in with Google retain their Google Account UID.
  • Display name — a name you choose within the App (not your real name unless you enter one).
  • Country — selected voluntarily by you for leaderboard purposes.
  • Game scores & statistics — best scores per difficulty, win/loss counts, best times, and total score used for leaderboards and your profile.

Device & advertising data

  • Advertising ID (GAID) — collected by Google AdMob to serve and personalise advertisements. You may reset or opt out of ad personalisation at any time via your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • General device information — device model, OS version, language, and network type, collected automatically by Firebase Analytics and AdMob for crash reporting, analytics, and ad delivery.
We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, or any other sensitive personal information.

How We Use Your Information

  • Game functionality — storing your profile, scores, and settings so they persist across sessions and devices.
  • Leaderboards — displaying your display name, country flag, and best score in world, country, and continent rankings.
  • Analytics — understanding how players interact with the App (which difficulty levels are popular, session lengths) so we can improve it. Analytics data is aggregated and not linked to personally identifiable information.
  • Advertising — showing banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob to support free access to the App.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use your Advertising ID and device information to personalise ads based on your interests.

Before any ads are loaded, the App presents a consent form (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform) in compliance with the GDPR and similar regulations. You can choose whether to allow personalised or non-personalised advertising.

For more information about how Google uses data for advertising, visit policies.google.com/technologies/ads .

To opt out of interest-based advertising, visit adssettings.google.com or adjust your device's advertising settings.

Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) to collect anonymised usage statistics such as screen views, session duration, and feature engagement. This data helps us improve the App experience.

Firebase Analytics data is processed by Google on our behalf and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy . You can disable analytics data collection in the App's device notification settings, or by enabling Limit Ad Tracking on your device.

Data Storage & Security

Your profile and score data are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, located in the europe-west2 (London) region. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google Cloud.

A local copy of your profile is also cached on your device using Hive (a local NoSQL database) for offline access. This data remains on your device only and is removed when you uninstall the App.

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure. We encourage you not to enter sensitive personal information as your display name or motto.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

We share data only with the following service providers, solely to operate the App:

  • Google LLC — Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics, and Google AdMob. Google processes this data as a data processor on our behalf.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users or the public.

Your Rights — GDPR (EU / EEA / UK Users)

If you are located in the European Union, European Economic Area, or United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data by updating your profile within the App.
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing of your data for direct marketing or where we rely on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw ad-personalisation consent at any time via the in-app consent form or your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Legal basis for processing: Performance of the App's core features (contract), your consent for personalised advertising, and our legitimate interest in improving the App via anonymised analytics.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us immediately and we will delete that information as quickly as possible.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you within the App.
