How to Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Sudoku Game account and all associated data, please send an email to the address below. Include the word "DELETE" in the subject line so we can process your request promptly.

tomic.support@gmail.com

What Gets Deleted

Upon receiving and verifying your request, we will permanently delete the following data within 30 days:

  • Firebase account — your authentication record and unique user ID.
  • Profile data — display name, country, motto, avatar, and all preferences.
  • Scores & statistics — best scores, best times, win/loss counts, total score, and leaderboard entries.

What Is Not Affected

  • Google Account — deleting your Sudoku Game account does not affect your Google Account in any way.
  • Aggregated analytics — anonymised, non-identifiable analytics data (e.g. session counts, feature usage) may be retained as it cannot be linked back to you.

Local Data

Data cached on your device (via Hive local storage) is removed automatically when you uninstall the App. If you wish to clear it without uninstalling, you can do so via your device's Settings → Apps → Sudoku Game → Clear Data.

Questions?

For any questions about data deletion or your privacy rights, please refer to our Privacy Policy or contact us at tomic.support@gmail.com.
